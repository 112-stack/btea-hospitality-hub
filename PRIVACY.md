# Hospitality Services Companion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 August 2026

Hospitality Services Companion is an independent, source-attributed guide and local preparation tool. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Bahrain Tourism and Exhibitions Authority (BTEA) or the Government of Bahrain.

## Data collected by the developer

The app does not transmit personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, diagnostics, or tracking data to the independent developer. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social SDK, account system, or developer-operated remote database.

## Data stored on the device

The app can store service favorites, synthetic applications, document names and sizes, inspection checklists, complaint working copies, accessibility settings, outlet records, and campaign drafts locally on the device. File bytes selected for a working copy are not uploaded by the app.

## Official external services

Official BTEA sources, eKey authentication, payments, inspections, and complaint submission open outside the companion. Those services are controlled by their respective operators and governed by their own terms and privacy notices. The companion does not receive official credentials, payment information, or regulatory results.

## Device permissions

The 2.1.0 release does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, media, advertising ID, notifications, or tracking permission. It reads network availability to explain when external sources can be opened and may use device haptics for optional touch feedback.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until the user resets the demonstration data, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. There is no companion account or remote developer-held record to delete. A reset control is available under **More > Reset local app data**.

## Children

The app is a business and government-information preparation utility. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's information.

## Security

The Android release disables cleartext network traffic and cloud backup of local drafts. External official links use HTTPS. Native builds request only the network permission required to open HTTPS sources.

## Contact

Questions about this independent project can be submitted through the [project support tracker](https://github.com/112-stack/btea-hospitality-hub/issues). Questions about official BTEA services should be directed to [BTEA's official contact page](https://portal.btea.bh/MainP/ContactUs).

Material changes to this policy will update the effective date and the app-store declarations before release.
