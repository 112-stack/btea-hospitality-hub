# App Review notes

Hospitality Services Companion is an independent, source-attributed utility. It is not an official BTEA or Government of Bahrain app.

No account is required. Reviewers can exercise the complete bundled workflow without credentials:

1. Open **Services**, search or filter the 19 service areas, and open a service.
2. Tap **Start local working copy** to create an on-device application.
3. Open **Cases** to advance the demonstration timeline and attach file metadata. No file bytes leave the device.
4. Open **Inspections** and toggle preparation checklist items.
5. Open **More > Complaints**, enter synthetic information, create the local verification code, and use `2468`. This does not send SMS or contact BTEA.
6. Open **More > Privacy & data controls** to review retention and deletion behavior.

Official eKey, payment, complaint, inspection, and service-source actions open external HTTPS pages and are clearly labelled. They are not required to review the companion. No payment or purchase is available in the app.

The app requests no sensitive system permission, contains no advertising or tracking SDK, and has no user-generated public content.
